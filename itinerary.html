Itinerary

Read through our itinerary to get an idea of what the wedding week is going to look like.

Wednesday, October 18th

Travel day

The wedding party arrives in San Diego.

Thursday, October 19th

Bachelor/bachelorette parties!

In the morning, the guys and the girls separate and do a daytime activity with each other and have lunch afterwards. At the end of the day, both parties will gather back together for dinner and drinks!

Friday, October 20th

Surf day & rehearsal dinner

During the day, we will go to the beach and have a surf day with the crew. We will be renting a couple of foamie surf boards for the beginners to catch some waves too!

All other guests arrive in San Diego.

In the late afternoon, the wedding party will meet at the ceremony location in Torrey Pines and practice walking in and out & standing arrangement.

At the end of the day, we will be providing a catered dinner from a local Mexican restaurant and beer and wine.

The Groom and Groomsmen will end the night at the second airbnb where they will spend the night and get ready on Wedding Day.
